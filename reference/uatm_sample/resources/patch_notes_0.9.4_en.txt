0.9.4 Release Notes

1) Congestion
- Added a congestion overlay with directional intensity based on speed ratio.
- Segmented corridors into 100 m bins for visualization.
- Aggregation windows: 1 / 5 / 10 / 30 minutes. Freeflow defaults: vertiport links 50 kt, others 100 kt.
- Congestion history now persists when toggling the layer.

2) Density
- Added a density overlay with wide lane rendering and right-hand traffic offsets.
- Directional density labels per link.
- Layer toggles with smooth fade transitions.

3) Fixes
- Improved overlay responsiveness and reduced label flicker.
- Improved label decluttering while moving/zooming the map.
- Ensured base lane (light/white) rendering appears consistently.
