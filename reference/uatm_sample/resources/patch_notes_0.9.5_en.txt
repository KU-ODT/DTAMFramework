0.9.5 Release Notes

1) Vertiport visualization stability
- Stabilized the rendering flow for intermittent cases where vertiports and vertiport links disappeared after startup/reset.
- Improved layer/source refresh sequencing during data re-apply to reduce missing visualization cases.

2) Vertiport management popup
- Clicking a vertiport now opens the management popup together with the vertiport zone (MTR).
- Added a horizontal two-pane layout.
- Left pane: vertiport info. Right pane: arrival/departure schedule template (Gate/FATO).

3) Popup interaction consistency
- Clearing a vertiport zone now closes the management popup together.
- Re-click toggle behavior for the same vertiport selection is now consistent.

4) Dark/white readability improvements
- Improved theme-specific contrast for popup background/text/table styles.
- Kept readability consistent when switching between dark and white themes.
